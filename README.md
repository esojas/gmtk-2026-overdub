# OverDub!

A submission to GMTK Game Jam 2026 theme: **Count Down**

You play as a capybara who can summon clones from your previous playthroughs. Each level (after level1), you get a countdown on how long you can live. When it hits zero, you explode and repeat again, but now with your past self joining!

## Controls
- [WASD] — move
- [Space] — jump
